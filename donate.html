OUR MISSION

Connecting the world through learning

Learnivia is built around a simple idea: every student should have access to meaningful learning opportunities, regardless of where they live or what resources they have.

Through free tutoring, educational programs, workshops, and peer-to-peer learning, we help students develop their skills while connecting with people who can support their journey.

Your donation helps us continue creating accessible learning experiences and reach more learners with the support they need to learn, grow, and move forward.
