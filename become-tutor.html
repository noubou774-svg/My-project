Why Tutor with Learnivia?

✦

Give back through volunteering.

Volunteer your time by hosting free tutoring sessions for students around the world who need help with high-school math, SAT prep, and more. Your tutoring experience can also help you build valuable service experience.

↗

Build leadership experience.

Being a tutor helps you develop strong leadership, communication, and organizational skills while giving you meaningful experience supporting other learners.

✧

Receive support from experienced educators.

When you tutor with Learnivia, you become part of a community that has your back. Connect with tutors and educators who can help you improve your teaching techniques and grow your confidence.

◉

Meet learners from around the world.

Our tutor community connects students from different backgrounds and countries. Share ideas, learn from others, and build meaningful connections through education.

j
Schoolhouse Certification & Portfolio

Volunteer as a tutor and build a college-ready portfolio.

As a tutor, you’ll build a verified Schoolhouse Portfolio that showcases your subject expertise and tutoring experience. It’s recognized by more than 50 colleges and universities as part of their admissions process.

How it Works

🎓

Get certified and apply

Before you host a tutoring session on Learnivia, you'll need to get certified in one or more subjects. Choose the topics you're confident in, complete a short application, and tell us a little about yourself.

Get trained to tutor

Once your application is approved, complete a set of training sessions at your own pace. These trainings will help you become a confident and effective tutor.

⭐
🌎

Join the community

As you prepare to host your first session, you'll join our vibrant Learnivia community. Connect with other tutors, exchange ideas, and get support from students and educators around the world.

Host your first session

Finally, host your first session! Choose a subject you're certified in, select a date and time, and start helping another learner reach their goals.

💻
🚀

Receive ongoing support

Your journey doesn't stop after your first session. Learnivia provides ongoing support, resources, and a community of tutors and educators who are here to help you grow.
